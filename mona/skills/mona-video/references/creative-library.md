# Creative source library

Use the Huashu Motion [library](huashu-motion/library/README.md),
[director](huashu-motion/director.md), [techniques](huashu-motion/library/techniques.md)
and [product continuity](huashu-motion/product-demo-continuity.md).

The selected built-in style exposes source.template. Copy its actual
assets/huashu-motion/templates/<template>/index.html into this project's drafts
as a baseline. Matching runtime/ and fonts/ are bundled. Source template names
are implementation details, not extra user-facing genre choices.

- Mechanisms and reasoning: mechanism, system-diagram, flat-world, hand-drawn.
- Product operations: cursor-demo, continuous-morph, card-montage and 3D space.
- Data and evidence: data-story and editorial-collage.
- A claim or opening: kinetic-type, with a content-specific main action.

Apply visual style and motion language separately. A whiteboard can explain a
mechanism; a product workflow can use the user's own palette. Templates are
starting source, not fixed stories or a restricted scene menu. Follow Mona's
one director confirmation and managed production contract. The complete old
independent Dashi and huashu-art-motion copies are no longer active resources.
